# Arithmetic Operators – HackerRank

**Problem:** Given two integers `a` and `b`, print the following three results on separate lines:

1. The sum of the two integers (`a + b`).
2. The difference between the first and second integers (`a - b`).
3. The product of the two integers (`a * b`).

**Input Format:**
- The first line contains integer `a`.
- The second line contains integer `b`.

**Output Format:**
Print the sum, difference, and product on three separate lines.

**Sample Input**
```text
3
2
```

**Sample Output**
```text
5
1
6
```
